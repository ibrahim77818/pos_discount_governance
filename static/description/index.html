POS Discount Governance

Give line and global discounts as a percentage or a fixed amount, exact to the cent on the order, the invoice and the session entry. Every employee gets a discount level, anything above it needs a supervisor’s PIN or badge, and the server re-checks every order before accepting it.

Why this module

Discounts are where tills leak money. Standard Odoo lets you switch manual discounts on or off, but not decide who may give how much, or prove afterwards who approved what.

In standard Odoo 17

  • The % Disc button only takes a percentage: “40 SR off” must be converted by hand.
  • The global Discount button (pos_discount) is also percentage only.
  • A hand-made percentage rarely lands on the cent, and the invoice rounds the discount to 2 decimals.
  • No limit per employee: anyone allowed to discount can give 100%.
  • No supervisor approval step for a large discount.
  • The order keeps a discount percentage, but not who gave or approved it.

With this module

  • Percentage or Fixed Amount in one popup, with a live preview of the discount.
  • Global discounts in both modes, split across tax groups so VAT stays right.
  • Amounts are solved against Odoo’s own tax engine: order, invoice and session match to the cent.
  • Discount levels with a maximum % per discount and an optional amount cap per order.
  • Above the limit: Supervisor Override by PIN, badge, or both, on the spot.
  • A Discount Audit Log on every order, re-checked by the server and tamper-resistant.

How it works

Four steps, all at the till, with no extra screens for the cashier.

1
Set levels
Create discount levels and give each employee one, next to their PIN and badge.
2
Enter the discount
The cashier presses % Disc or Discount and enters a percentage or an amount.
3
Approve if needed
Within the limit it applies at once. Above it, a supervisor approves with PIN or badge.
4
Server re-checks
On sync, the server verifies every approval and stores the audit log with the order.

Features

Screenshots use a demo shop: Abigail Peterson (Cashier level, 5%), Doris Cole (Supervisor level, 50%), and the products DG Coffee Machine (1,000 SR + 15% VAT), DG Gift Box (50 SR, no tax) and DG Mug (33.33 SR + 15% VAT).

Menus

Where everything lives

Point of Sale › Configuration › Discount Levels to manage the levels, and Point of Sale › Reporting › Discount Audit Log to review every discount. Both menus are for POS managers.

Feature 1

Discount levels that match your staff

Each level sets a Max Discount (%) per discount and an optional Max Discount Amount per Order (0 means no amount cap), and allows or forbids Fixed Amount Discounts and Global Discounts. Levels with Can Approve Overrides are your supervisors. Four levels ship ready to edit: Cashier 5%, Senior Cashier 15%, Supervisor 50% and Store Manager 100%.

Feature 2

One field on the employee

On the employee form, HR Settings tab, POS Discount Level sits right next to the PIN Code and Badge ID that pos_hr already uses. Employees without a level get the POS Default Discount Level. An employee whose level can approve overrides must have a PIN.

Feature 3

Percentage or fixed amount on any line

% Disc opens the Line Discount popup: choose Percentage or Fixed Amount, type or tap the value, and see the result before applying, together with the tax policy and the cashier’s own limit.

Worked example: 40 SR off the coffee machine, before tax. The untaxed amount drops from 1,000.00 to 960.00 SR, so the line becomes 1,104.00 SR with VAT. The module stores the exact percentage behind it, so a 0.05 SR discount on 1,000 × 10.05 still gives exactly 10,049.95 on the order and the invoice. Change the quantity and the fixed amount stays fixed.
Feature 4

Supervisor override, right at the till

Abigail tries 20% on the mug; her limit is 5%. The till explains why (Discount Above Your Limit) and offers an Override. Only employees whose level can approve and covers this discount are listed. A wrong PIN is refused and the field is cleared; after 5 wrong attempts verification locks for 60 seconds.

Badge scanners work as keyboards in the badge field, so scanning a badge never switches the logged-in cashier by mistake.
Feature 5

Global discounts split by tax group

The Discount button gets the same popup for the whole order. The discount is split over the order’s tax groups in proportion to their amounts, and the parts always add up to the exact total. A level without Allow Global Discounts is sent to a supervisor.

Split: 10% of the order is 98.02 SR before tax: 93.17 on the 15% VAT group (931.66) and 4.85 on the untaxed gift box (48.50). VAT is reduced only where VAT was charged.
Feature 6

A Discount Audit Log on every order

The order form gets a Discount Audit Log tab: timestamp, scope (Line / Global), mode, tax policy, product, entered value, discount amount, cashier, Authorized By, Authorization Method, Supervisor Override and Credentials Verified. Overrides are highlighted.

Feature 7

The server has the last word

When an order syncs, the server recomputes every discount from the order lines and checks it against its own approval. Choose what happens on a violation: Reject the order (checked before an order number is used) or Accept and flag the order, which adds a red Discount Violation ribbon, the reason, and the Discount Violations filter on the orders list.

Feature 8

Review discounts across sessions

Reporting › Discount Audit Log lists every discount, with filters Supervisor Overrides, Unverified Credentials, Line Discounts, Global Discounts and Today, grouping by authorizer, cashier, session or day, and a pivot of discount amounts per approver.

Tamper resistance: after a successful PIN or badge check, the till sends a proof that can only be produced with the plain credential, bound to the order, the line, the product and the approved value. The server recomputes it from the employee record. Changing the value, or copying the approval to another line or order, breaks it. Audit entries are created by the server only; POS users can read them, not create or edit them.

Built for real-world corner cases

What happens at a busy till, handled in the code and covered by tests.

Invoices to the cent
The invoice line keeps the exact POS discount, so a 0.0005% discount is not rounded to 0.00%.
Tax-included prices
Before-tax and after-tax policies work for tax-included and tax-excluded products alike.
Quantity changes
A fixed line discount keeps its amount when the quantity changes; a percentage scales with it.
Refunds and rewards
Refund lines and loyalty reward lines are not counted as new discounts.
Several discounts per order
An earlier supervisor approval never blocks a later discount inside the cashier’s own limit.
No gaps in order numbers
In Reject mode the order is checked before it is created, so rejected orders do not use a number.
Managers without a PIN
An approver-level employee without a PIN only gets the default level when acting as cashier.
Arabic keyboards
Arabic-Indic digits and the Arabic decimal mark are accepted; amounts stay left-to-right in RTL.
Offline and restaurant drafts
Checks need no server call, and a re-synced draft order replaces its log instead of duplicating it.
